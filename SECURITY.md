📑 Securities Policy – Greenlight / MDP Holdings Trust & LLC
This document defines how Greenlight and its affiliates handle securities compliance, investor protections, and disclosure standards. It is intended for regulators, investors, and stakeholders who require clarity on our governance and compliance posture.

⚖️ Compliance Framework
• 	Greenlight operates under SkunkwurX Owned By MDP Holdings Group
• 	All offerings are structured in accordance with U.S. federal and state securities laws.
• 	Depending on the instrument, exemptions may be filed under Regulation D (Rule 506(b)/(c)), Regulation CF, or Regulation A+.
• 	No token, share, or financial instrument issued by Greenlight should be construed as an unregistered security unless explicitly filed and disclosed.

📊 Investor Disclosures
• 	Risk Factors – All investments carry risk, including loss of principal.
• 	Use of Proceeds – Allocations are documented in offering memoranda and audited where required.
• 	Financial Reporting – Investors receive periodic updates, including reviewed or audited financials.
• 	Material Events – Mergers, acquisitions, litigation, or regulatory actions are disclosed promptly.
• 	Governance – Investor rights, voting structures, and redemption policies are defined in offering documents.

🔐 Digital Asset & Token Policy
• 	Ecosystem tokens (e.g., $MAD, $MADMONEY) are designed primarily for utility and ecosystem participation.
• 	Where tokens may be deemed securities under the Howey Test, Greenlight will file or exempt offerings accordingly.
• 	Validator operations, staking pools, and ecosystem rewards are documented with timestamped compliance artifacts.
• 	All tokenomics, vesting schedules, and distribution mechanics are published for investor review.
